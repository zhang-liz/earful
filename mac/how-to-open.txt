如何打开 Earful
================

1. 把 Earful 拖到右边的“应用程序”文件夹。
2. 打开“应用程序”文件夹，双击 Earful。
   第一次会弹出提示，说 Apple 无法验证 Earful。
   这是因为 Earful 没有向苹果付费登记，不是因为它有问题。点“完成”（不要点“移到废纸篓”）。
3. 打开“系统设置” >“隐私与安全性”，一直往下滚动。
   在“安全性”下面，会看到一行说 Earful 被阻止了。点旁边的“仍要打开”。
4. 输入你的 Mac 登录密码，再点一次“仍要打开”。
5. 以后直接双击 Earful 就能用，不用再做这些步骤。

Earful 打开后，浏览器会自动打开朗读页面。
Earful 在你自己的电脑上朗读，不需要联网，你的文字不会发到任何地方。
程序坞里的 Earful 图标亮着时，它就在工作。退出 Earful，它就停止。

需要 Apple 芯片（M1 或更新）的 Mac，macOS 13 或更新版本。


How to open Earful
==================

1. Drag Earful to the Applications folder on the right.
2. Open the Applications folder and double-click Earful.
   The first time, macOS says Apple could not verify Earful. This is
   because Earful is not registered with Apple, which costs money. It is
   not a problem with Earful. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, and scroll down.
   Under "Security", a line says Earful was blocked.
   Click "Open Anyway" next to it.
4. Type your Mac password, and click "Open Anyway" again.
5. From then on, double-click Earful to use it. No more steps.

When Earful opens, your browser opens the reading page.
Earful reads on your own computer, with no internet. Your text goes nowhere.
While the Earful icon in the Dock is lit, it is working. Quit Earful to stop it.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later.
