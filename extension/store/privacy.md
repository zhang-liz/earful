# Earful privacy policy · Earful 隐私政策

## English

Earful does not collect, keep or share any personal data.

- The text you ask Earful to read goes only to the Earful app at the address
  in the extension's settings. By default that is your own computer
  (127.0.0.1), and it never leaves it.
- The app turns the text into sound on that computer, and keeps the
  finished sound there.
- The extension saves its settings (voices, speed, language, the app's
  address, and a password if you set one) in your browser's settings
  storage. If you turn on sync in your browser, your browser copies them to
  your other devices, as it does for other extensions.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no ads, no
  analytics and no tracking.

## 中文

Earful 不收集、不保存、也不分享任何个人信息。

- 你让 Earful 朗读的文字，只会发给扩展程序设置里填写的 Earful 应用。默认就是你自己的电脑（127.0.0.1），文字不会离开这台电脑。
- Earful 应用在这台电脑上把文字变成声音，生成的声音也保存在这台电脑上。
- 扩展程序把设置（声音、语速、语言、应用地址，以及你设置的密码）保存在浏览器的设置存储里。如果你在浏览器里开启了同步，浏览器会像对其他扩展程序一样，把这些设置同步到你的其他设备。
- 不会向开发者或任何其他人发送任何内容。没有广告，没有统计，没有跟踪。
